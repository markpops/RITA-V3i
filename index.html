Backup & Portability

All data stays on this device (browser storage). Safari may clear it, so export a backup regularly and keep it in your own secure storage. Add RITA to the Home Screen for more reliable storage.

Patient photos and records are personal health data. Keep backups encrypted, follow your institution's rules and data protection law (KVKK), and obtain patient consent.
